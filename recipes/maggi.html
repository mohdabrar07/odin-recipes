Maggi

MAggi , is an easy to snack within 4 to 5 minutes.

Ingredients:

  • Maggi noodles
  • Spicy maggi powder
  • Water
  • Onion, Tomato,and Green chilly chopped. (Additional)

Steps:

  1. Keep a pan of water and heat (approx 250ml per person).
  2. Add maggi , maggi powder.
  3. Add choped onion ,tomato, green chilly for better taste.
  4. Boil it for 3 to 5 minutes until its ready to serve.
  5. Ready to serve.
