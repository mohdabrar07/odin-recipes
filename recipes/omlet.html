Omlet Recipe

Omlet , is an easy to cook dish within 4 to 5 minutes.

Ingredients:

  • Egg
  • Salt
  • Pepper powder
  • onion
  • Tomato
  • Green chilly

Steps:

  1. Break an egg into a bowl
  2. Add a pinch of salt
  3. Add half chopped onion, tomato and green chilly.
  4. Stir and mix well with spoon or stirring tool.
  5. Heat a pan with a few drops of oil.
  6. Pour the mixed ingredients to pan.
  7. After omlet is half boiled sprinkle the pepper powder.
  8. Heat both the sides if necessary .
  9. Ready to serve .
